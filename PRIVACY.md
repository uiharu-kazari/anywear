# Anywear — Privacy

_Last updated: 2026-11-17_

This policy covers **Anywear for Anna AI OS** (the App in the Anna App Store). If you run your own copy of the open-source web version, you are the operator and the services you configure (e.g. Perfect Corp YouCam, Google Gemini) apply instead.

## What Anywear handles

- A full-body photo of you (your "twin") used for virtual try-on
- An optional bare-faced selfie used for a visual skin read
- Screenshots or photos of clothing you drop or paste in
- The results: try-on images, skin scores and brief, stylist verdicts, your lookbook and chosen occasion

## Where it goes

- **No Anywear server.** The Anna edition has no backend of its own. The developer does not receive, see or store your photos or results.
- **AI processing runs through Anna.** Skin read, garment detection, skin brief and stylist verdict are sent to the AI model you use in Anna. Try-on images are generated by Anna's image-generation service (by default Google's Nano Banana model, served via Anna's provider fal.ai). To do that, Anna temporarily hosts the two input images (your twin photo and the garment crop) in its storage. This processing is billed to your Anna credits and is governed by [Anna's terms and privacy policy](https://anna.partners).
- **Your data stays in your Anna account.** Your photos, results and lookbook are saved in Anywear's private storage inside your Anna account so they're there next time you open the App. Other Apps cannot read them.

## What Anywear does not do

- No ads, analytics, tracking pixels or third-party SDKs
- No selling or sharing of your data
- No use of your photos to train models by the developer

## Your choices

- **Start fresh** (top-right in the App) clears your twin photo, selfie, results and lookbook. Removed images are then deleted from Anywear's storage in your Anna account.
- Uninstalling the App from Anna, or deleting your Anna account, is handled by Anna under its own data-retention rules.

## Not medical advice

The skin read is a visual styling estimate made by an AI model. It is not a medical or dermatological assessment.
